# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 15, 2026*

## 1. Who we are

Askit Platform Private Limited ("AskIt", "we", "us", "our") operates the website at askitindia.com (the "Website"). AskIt connects businesses with vetted student creative talent. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through the Website and how you can control it.

Registered office: Flat No. 15/9, Pusa Road, Karol Bagh, New Delhi, Delhi 110005, India

## 2. What this Policy covers

This Policy covers the Website, which is currently an informational site. The Website does not store or send us anything you type into it, other than emails you choose to send us.

The AskIt app at askitindia.com/app is separate from the Website. If you register or apply there, the information you provide is covered by the notices and terms shown in the app.

## 3. What we collect

**Emails you send us.** The contact buttons on the Website open your own email app so you can write to support@askitindia.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your name, phone number, or message.

**Review form and chatbot.** The "Share Your Experience" form and the chatbot on the Website currently work only inside your browser. What you type is not sent to us, saved, or recorded.

**Technical data.** Like most websites, our hosting and security providers, Cloudflare and Lovable, automatically process basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and timestamps. This is used to deliver and secure the Website and isn't used by us to build profiles of visitors.

We don't ask for sensitive information, such as government ID numbers, financial account details, or health information, through the Website.

## 4. How we use it

We use emails you send us to respond to you and follow up about your enquiry. We don't send unrelated marketing, and we won't start without giving you a clear way to opt out. Contacting us is an expression of interest only. It doesn't create a contract, and it doesn't guarantee onboarding, work, or any other outcome.

## 5. Who we share it with

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

We use these providers to run the Website:

- **Lovable**: hosts the Website
- **Cloudflare**: provides security and fast delivery for the Website

They only process data to help us operate the Website, and may process it on servers located outside India as part of their standard infrastructure.

We may also share information where we're legally required to, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of AskIt, in which case this Policy would carry over or you'd be notified of a new one.

## 6. Cookies and local storage

The Website saves your light or dark theme choice in local storage on your device. This preference contains no personal identifiers and is not used to track you. The Website does not currently use the expandable sidebar that would save a menu-state cookie. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

**Visitor analytics.** We use Lovable's visitor analytics to record pages visited, where visitors came from, country, browser, operating system and device type, time on the Website, page-load speed, errors, and clicks on buttons and links. With no consent banner currently connected, these measurements are recorded for every visitor. Page visits, time on page, page speed and errors are recorded without cookies and without identifying anyone.

Lovable's analytics uses no cookies, stores nothing on your device, does not keep IP addresses and does not identify visitors. Detailed data is kept for 90 days and daily summaries for one year. Lovable collects this data for us. [Lovable's privacy policy](https://lovable.dev/privacy) explains how it handles it.

**Fonts and external services.** The Website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google your IP address, browser request information and the font requests needed to display the Website. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Contact links open your email app; your email provider and our email provider process the email you send. Social media, app and tutorial links take you to separate services whose privacy notices apply when you open them.

## 7. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect information we receive, including limiting access to team members who need it and using access-controlled service providers. No online system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep emails you send us for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and follow up, generally up to 24 months if we haven't moved forward together, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. If we do work together, we keep relevant information for as long as needed for that relationship, and afterwards as required by law.

## 9. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, stop contacting you, or withdraw your consent, at any time, by emailing support@askitindia.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

## 10. Children

AskIt is intended for people 18 and older: college and university students and business representatives. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has contacted us, email support@askitindia.com so we can delete it.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We will update this Policy if the Website starts collecting more information. We'll post the latest version here with a new "last updated" date.
